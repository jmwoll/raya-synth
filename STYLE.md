# raya default style

Modules sit on a deep near-black canvas washed with a slow teal-to-forest-green
gradient, where faint luminous filaments drift like particle trails, alive but
never distracting. Modules are matte charcoal panels with subtle bevels, grouped
in clear rows, framed by thin warm-wood side cheeks for a tactile, instrument-like
feel. Controls are small dark knobs with crisp white indicator lines, plus
minimal toggles and faders. Patch cables hang in soft curves with muted colours:
amber, rose, sage, dusty blue. Scopes glow phosphor green. Typography is sparse,
uppercase, widely tracked, clean white sans-serif. Accent glow is soft green;
everything else stays calm, dim and spacious.
